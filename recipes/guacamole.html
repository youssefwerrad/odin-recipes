Guacamole

Home

Description

Fresh, creamy guacamole made with ripe avocados, lime, and cilantro. Simple to make and perfect with tortilla chips or as a topping for tacos.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • 1/2 red onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Halve the avocados, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  2. Mash the avocado with a fork to your desired consistency.
  3. Stir in onion, cilantro, lime juice, tomato, and garlic.
  4. Season with salt to taste.
  5. Serve immediately with tortilla chips.
