Pancakes

Home

Description

Fluffy homemade pancakes made from scratch — soft, golden, and perfect with maple syrup and butter. A classic breakfast that comes together in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Steps

  1. In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  2. In another bowl, whisk milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry and stir until just combined (a few lumps are fine).
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle over medium heat.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden.
  6. Serve warm with syrup and butter.
