Description

Classic lasagna is a layered pasta bake made with sheets of lasagna noodles, a rich meat and tomato sauce, and a creamy blend of cheeses. It's baked until bubbly and golden on top — comfort food at its finest, perfect for feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 oz marinara sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions; drain.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef with onion and garlic. Add marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, mix ricotta, egg, and half the Parmesan.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella, and more meat sauce. Repeat.
  6. Top with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake 15 more minutes until golden.
  8. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.
